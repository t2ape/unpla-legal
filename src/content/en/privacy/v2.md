The person who provides the Service (the "Operator") establishes this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") as set out below regarding the handling of User Information, including personal information about Users, in the iOS application named "UnplaQuest" provided by the Operator and its related services (the "Service").

The Service is an application that helps Users reduce their own smartphone use. The Service respects Users' privacy. To that end, it is designed so that, as a rule, information relating to Screen Time, including information about which applications a User has used and for how long, is handled only on the User's device and is not sent to the Operator's servers (see Article 1, paragraphs 4 and 5 for details).

In this Policy, "User" means an individual who uses the Service, whether registered or not (including a person who uses the Service by downloading it, by going through onboarding (initial setup), or in any other way). Other terms used in this Policy that are defined in the Terms of Service for the Service (the "Terms of Service") have the same meanings in this Policy as in the Terms of Service.

## Article 1 (User Information Collected and How It Is Collected)

In this Policy, "User Information" means information that identifies a User, a User's activity history in the Service, and other information generated or accumulated in relation to a User or a User's device, that the Operator collects under this Policy. The User Information that the Operator collects in the Service is as follows, according to how it is collected.

1. Information provided by Users

    The information that Users provide in order to use the Service, or through the use of the Service, is as follows.

    - Email address (if the User chooses authentication by email address)
    - Answers to the diagnostic questions in the onboarding (initial setup) of the Service about smartphone use, usage habits, age group, and similar matters
    - Game-related information that the User enters or sets in the Service (including progress in raising characters, battle records, streak days, and other settings)

2. Information provided by another service when the User permits a link with that service

    If a User uses "Sign in with Apple" (signing in with an Apple Account) to use the Service, the Operator collects the following information from Apple Inc. ("Apple") based on what the User agreed to when giving that permission.

    - The User identifier issued by Apple
    - Email address (if the User permits it to be provided; if the User uses Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, the relay email address issued by Apple)

3. Information collected by the Operator when Users use the Service

    The Operator may collect information about how the Service is accessed and used. This includes the following information, which is collected automatically through the Service and the information collection modules set out in Article 3.

    - Information about usage of the Service and operation history (in-app events such as progress through onboarding, goal setting, the start and end of focus (usage restriction) sessions, the start, end, and results of battles, level-ups, updates to streak days, the display of and interaction with review prompts, the display of the purchase screen for the Paid Service, and the start and completion of purchases, and information accompanying those events)
    - Device type, OS type and version, version of the Service, language and region settings, and other information about the device
    - IP address
    - Identifiers assigned to the User within the Service (including the user ID)
    - Information about defects and crashes of the Service (such as the state at the time of a crash, error details, and device and OS information)

4. Information that the Service handles only on the User's device and does not send to the Operator's servers

    The Service uses the Screen Time features provided by Apple (such as DeviceActivity and FamilyControls) to measure the usage of applications specified by the User, restrict their use, and determine whether goals are met. Because of the way those features work, the following information is processed and stored on the User's device and is not sent to the Operator's servers.

    - Information about which applications the User has used and for how long (including the usage time of each application)
    - Information identifying the individual applications that the User has selected for usage restrictions or goal setting (because of the way Apple's features work, those applications are handled on the device as tokens in a form whose content the Operator cannot identify)

    The Service may display this information to the User on their own device, but the information displayed is not sent to the Operator. In addition, in accordance with the conditions set by Apple, the Operator does not send this information off the User's device, does not provide it to the information collection module providers set out in Article 3 or to any other third party, and does not use it for advertising or for any purpose other than providing the Service.

5. Information that the Operator receives in connection with Screen Time and goal setting

    Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, the information that the Operator receives in connection with Screen Time and goal setting, and stores on its servers or collects for analysis through the information collection modules set out in Article 3, is limited to aggregate or result information that does not identify individual applications, such as the following. The Operator does not know which applications a User uses or how long they are used.

    - The result of determining whether goals are met (including the number of applications for which the goal was met and the number of applications monitored)
    - Information outlining the goals set by the User (such as the number of applications selected for usage restrictions or goal setting, and the usage limits (time) set; this does not include information identifying which applications were selected)

6. Information about purchases of the Paid Service

    If a User purchases the Paid Service of the Service, the Operator receives information such as the following from Apple and from the provider of the billing management tool set out in Article 3.

    - The product purchased (type of plan), and the date, time, and type of each purchase, renewal, cancellation, refund, and the like
    - The expiration date of the Paid Service, and whether a free trial period has been used
    - Identifiers relating to transactions on the App Store and other information for verifying purchases (receipt information)
    - The country or region, currency, and price at the time of purchase

    Payment for the Paid Service is processed by Apple, and the Operator does not obtain credit card numbers or other information about payment methods. For Apple's handling of information, please see Apple's privacy policy.

## Article 2 (Purposes of Use)

The specific purposes for which User Information relating to the Service is used are as follows.

1. To accept registrations for the Service, authenticate Users, record User settings, and otherwise provide, maintain, protect, and improve the Service
2. To store game-related information such as Users' progress in raising characters, battle records, and streak days, and to provide the game-like features of the Service
3. To measure and analyze usage of the Service and User behavior, and to improve the content, features, and user experience of the Service
4. To detect, analyze, and respond to defects and crashes of the Service
5. To provide information about the Service and to respond to inquiries and the like
6. To respond to conduct that violates the Operator's Terms of Service, this Policy, and the like relating to the Service (the "Terms and Policies")
7. To give notice of changes to the Terms and Policies relating to the Service and the like
8. To provide the Paid Service, restore purchases, verify and manage purchase and billing status (including whether a User has the right to use the Paid Service), analyze purchase status, and handle related matters
9. For purposes incidental to the purposes of use above

## Article 4 (Provision to Third Parties)

1. The Operator does not provide personal information included in User Information to a third party without the User's prior consent. However, this does not apply where it is necessary to provide the information to a third party in the following cases.
    1. Where the Operator entrusts all or part of the handling of personal information to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use
    2. Where personal information is provided as a result of the succession of the business through a merger or for another reason
    3. Where personal information is provided to an information collection module provider in accordance with Article 3
    4. In other cases permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan (the "APPI") or other laws and regulations
2. When the Operator provides personal information to a third party, and in other cases where the APPI requires it, the Operator will create and keep records in accordance with that Act.

1. The Operator takes necessary and appropriate measures to prevent the leakage, loss, or damage of User Information and otherwise to manage User Information securely. 2. When entrusting the handling of personal information included in User Information to a third party (including Supabase, Inc., the provider of the cloud service used as the infrastructure of the Service), the Operator selects a business that meets the standards set by the Operator and exercises necessary and appropriate supervision over that contractor's handling of personal information.
3. For the specific details of the security control measures taken by the Operator, please contact the contact point set out in Article 8.

## Article 7 (Requests for Disclosure and the Like of Personal Information)

1. If a User makes a request under the APPI for notification of the purposes of use of retained personal data, disclosure of retained personal data or of records of provision to third parties, correction of, addition to, or deletion of the content of retained personal data, or suspension of use of, erasure of, or suspension of provision to third parties of retained personal data (together, a "Request for Disclosure and the Like of Personal Information"), the Operator will respond without delay in accordance with laws and regulations after confirming that the request is made by the User in person. The Operator will respond in the same way when a User who lives outside Japan makes a similar request under the laws of the place where they live.
2. Please send a Request for Disclosure and the Like of Personal Information by email to the contact point set out in Article 8. To verify your identity, the Operator may ask you to contact it from the email address registered with the Service or to complete verification by another method specified by the Operator.
3. For requests under the preceding paragraph for notification of the purposes of use of retained personal data and for disclosure of retained personal data or of records of provision to third parties, the Operator charges a fee of 1,000 yen (including consumption tax) per request. The contact point set out in Article 8 will inform you how to pay the fee.
4. A User may withdraw from the Service and delete their account at any time by the prescribed operation in the Service. When an account is deleted, the information about the User stored on the Operator's servers is deleted. However, information that the Operator is legally required to keep, and purchase records that need to be kept to prevent fraudulent purchases and refunds or for another legitimate reason, may be retained only to the extent and for the period necessary.
5. Deleting an account does not cancel a Subscription to the Paid Service. Please cancel a Subscription by the method specified by Apple.
6. If you would like the information provided to the information collection module providers set out in Article 3 to be deleted, please contact the contact point set out in Article 8. The Operator will ask each provider to delete it.

## Article 9 (Use by Children)

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. If the Operator learns that it has obtained personal information from a child under the age of 13 without the consent of a parent or other legal representative, it will promptly delete that information. Users who are minors aged 13 or over must obtain the consent of a parent or other legal representative before using the Service.

The Operator will change this Policy as necessary. However, when making a change to this Policy that requires the consent of Users by law, the changed Policy applies only to Users who have agreed to the change by the method specified by the Operator. When changing this Policy, the Operator will make known, or notify Users of, the time at which the changed Policy takes effect and its content by displaying them in the Service or by another appropriate method.
