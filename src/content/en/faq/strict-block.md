---
question: I want stricter blocking
status: published
---

To make blocks harder to remove yourself, combine the following.

1. **Do not allow pausing or resetting**: When you create a "Block on a schedule" rule, turn off "Allow pausing the block" and the block cannot be removed during the schedule. When you create a "Limit by time" or "Limit by opens" rule, turn off "Allow resetting the limit" and the block cannot be removed until the next day once the limit applies.
2. **Take a deep breath before unblocking**: A screen that makes you wait a few seconds appears before you unblock or reset. It keeps you from unblocking on impulse. It is on by default. If you turned it off, you can turn it back on from the gear button at the top right of the confirmation screen for unblocking or resetting.
3. **Prevent app deletion**: Turn this on in the app's "Settings" and you can no longer remove blocks by deleting the app. Please note that, because of how iOS works, this prevents deleting every app on the device.
4. **Have someone else set your Screen Time passcode**: iOS Screen Time can require a passcode to change its settings. Ask a family member or friend to set this passcode in the iOS Settings app, and you will no longer be able to change the Screen Time settings yourself.
5. **Keep your phone physically out of reach**: You can also use a timed lock box for phones, which does not open until the time is up.
