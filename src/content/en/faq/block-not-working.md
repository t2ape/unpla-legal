---
question: A block does not work, or will not come off
category: trouble
order: 2
status: published
---

Blocks are applied and removed by syncing with the Screen Time settings on your device. The sync happens automatically, but it sometimes fails. Please try the following in order.

1. In the app, open "Settings" and tap "**Reload blocks**". This fixes it in most cases.
2. If it does not, **restart your device** and then tap "Reload blocks" again.
3. If it still does not, please email us from the [Support](/en/support) page. It helps us look into it if you include your device, your iOS version, and what happened with which block (it does not apply or does not come off, and since when).
